## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-09
### Added
- Penyesuaian penuh seluruh dokumen template (`GDD.md`, `TECH_STACK.md`, `CORE_MECHANICS.md`, `SPRINT.md`, `UI_FLOW.md`, `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`, `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, `RULES.md`, dan `.cursorrules`) untuk konsep **Gimersia: 3D Claymation Arcade Party Racing (6 Players)**.
- Spesifikasi arsitektur teknis menggunakan [Unity 3D (URP)](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.shadergraph@latest/), [Photon Fusion 2](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/v2/fusion-intro), *Custom Raycast Vehicle Physics*, [Unity Cinemachine](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.cinemachine@latest/), [Unity Shader Graph](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.shadergraph@latest/), [Unity UI Toolkit](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.4/Documentation/Manual/ui-systems/ui-shader-graph.html) + [DOTween](https://dotween.demigiant.com/), dan [FMOD for Unity](https://www.fmod.com/unity).
- Rumus matematika dan parameter *balancing* untuk **The "Weight" Item System** (*debuff* beban saat membawa item), mekanik **Self-Bounce Power-Up**, **Dual-Function Clay Wall** (*Blocker* & *Drift-Ramp Booster*), serta **Pre-Race RNG Dice Grid (1–6)**.
- Desain 3 Map utama: *Map 1: The Kitchen Counter*, *Map 2: The Clay Sculptor's Desk*, dan *Map 3: The Playground*.
